# Social-Graphs-and-interactions-of-The-Office-US

We study the connections between
characters in the popular TV series
’The Office’. More precisely, the
goal is to determine which out of
approximately 100 relevant charac-
ters is the main character across all
9 seasons of the series. Besides
that, we investigate how the overall
sentiment changes across all sea-
sons of the series, and who are the
most positive and negative charac-
ters in the series. The change of
sentiment across seasons is illus-
trated by comparing the overall and
sentiment of 3 sets of seasons.
